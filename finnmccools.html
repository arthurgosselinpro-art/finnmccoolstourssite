Our Story

Tours born from love of place.

🏰

Finn McCool's Tours began as a single coach and a wild idea: that the best way to see Ireland isn't through a polished script, but through stories told by people who actually live them.

Years on, we've consistently ranked among the top tour companies in Northern Europe, with over 40,000 five-star reviews across TripAdvisor, Viator, GetYourGuide and Google. But the thing that matters most to us hasn't changed: that you finish the day with a story worth telling.

We run modern, comfortable coaches with separate drivers and guides. We believe in free cancellation, fair prices, and giving you proper time at every stop — not just a photo-op rush.
